Spelling help

An orange box shows a guessed or unchecked spelling in grey. Type the spelling to confirm or correct it; clear the box to go back to the guess. A word’s spelling is shared by every card that uses it.

A dash breaks the stroke between syllables (ka-na-wi). An apostrophe splits letters that would otherwise join: owa is ow + a, o'wa is o + wa. A number after a letter picks an alternate form: lo2 turns the o the other way (lo\2 and lo/2 work too).

Use _ for a blank and *stars* for italics, in Chinook and English.
